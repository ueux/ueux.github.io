About

I build systems around intelligence

I'm an engineer focused on building AI-powered products, scalable backend systems, and intelligent infrastructure. I work at the intersection of software engineering and AI — from designing reliable APIs and distributed services to building LLM applications, RAG pipelines, agents, and intelligent automation.

I care about more than making a model work. I care about building systems that are fast, reliable, observable, scalable, and useful in the real world.

My goal is simple: turn complex ideas into reliable software that actually works. I'm particularly drawn to systems where traditional engineering and AI converge — intelligent backends, AI platforms, developer tools, autonomous systems, and eventually robotics.
